# 📱 QuoteIt

**Daily quotes, random quotes, and inspiring quotes by category — save your favorites and share beautiful quote images.**

---

## 📖 Description

Discover inspiration every day with **QuoteIt**! Explore quotes across different topics, get a fresh quote for the day, save quotes you love, and share your favorite quotes as beautiful images with friends and family or on social media.

QuoteIt is designed with a simple and clean interface so you can focus on discovering and sharing meaningful quotes without distractions.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💬 Explore Quotes

Browse and discover quotes across a variety of categories, including:

* Motivation
* Love
* Life
* Friendship
* Success
* Happiness
* And more

### 🌅 Today's Quote

Start your day with a fresh and inspiring **Quote of the Day**.

### 🎲 Random Quotes

Generate random quotes whenever you need a little inspiration or motivation.

### ❤️ Save Favorites

Save your favorite quotes so you can easily find and read them again later.

### 🖼️ Share as Image

Turn quotes into beautiful images and share them directly with your friends or on social media platforms such as WhatsApp and Instagram.

### 📱 Simple & Clean UI

Enjoy a minimal and easy-to-use interface built with **Jetpack Compose**.

### 📡 Offline Access

Access available quotes even when you don't have an internet connection.

## 🚀 How to Run

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/srprawinraja/QuoteIt.git
```

### 2. Open the project

Open the cloned project in **Android Studio**.

### 3. Sync Gradle

Allow Android Studio to sync the Gradle files and download the required dependencies.

### 4. Run the application

Connect an Android device or start an Android emulator.

Click **Run ▶️** in Android Studio to launch QuoteIt.

---

## ▶️ Usage

1. Open the **QuoteIt** app.
2. Browse quotes using the available categories.
3. Select a category to discover quotes on a specific topic.
4. Generate or explore random quotes whenever you need inspiration.
5. Use the available actions below a quote to:

    * ❤️ Save the quote to your favorites.
    * 🖼️ Share the quote as an image.
6. Open your saved favorites to revisit quotes you love.
7. Share your favorite quote images with friends or on social media.
